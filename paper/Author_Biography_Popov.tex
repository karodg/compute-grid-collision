\noindent \textbf{Biographical Sketch:} \\
\textbf{Danil Popov} is an independent researcher specializing in applied econometrics, energy economics, and industrial organization. His research agenda investigates the general-equilibrium interactions between technological revolutions, high-intensity computational infrastructure, wholesale electricity market design, and public utility regulation. His recent empirical work focuses on capacity remuneration mechanisms, quasi-experimental transmission boundary designs, and the distributional incidence of utility ratemaking.
